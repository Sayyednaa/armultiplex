Workflow Precision

Our 5-Step Fabrication Process

How we take your ideas from raw metal stock to fully installed, polished structures.

Step 01

Consultation & On-Site Measurement

We review your specifications or visit your Mumbai site to take exact millimeter dimensions, assessing load requirements and spatial layout.

Step 02

Material Selection & Precision Cutting

Raw SS sheets, MS beams, or aluminium extrusions are sourced in certified gauges and cut via heavy-duty shears, drop saws, and CNC punch presses.

Step 03

Master Argon & TIG/Arc Welding

Experienced craftsmen execute deep penetration argon welds with minimal heat distortion, creating ultra-strong, leakproof, and seamless joints.

Step 04

Grinding, Buffing & Surface Finishing

Weld beads are flushed and polished using multi-stage abrasive belts. SS products receive clean satin/hairline finish; MS pieces receive anti-rust primers.

Step 05

Quality Inspection & On-Site Installation

Every piece undergoes rigorous dimensional and load checks before safe transport and on-site alignment by our Sakinaka technician crew.
